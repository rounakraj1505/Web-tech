About FitTrack

FitTrack is a mobile application designed to help users build healthier habits. Whether you are a beginner or a seasoned athlete, FitTrack adapts to your goals and keeps you motivated every single day.

Our mission is simple: make fitness tracking effortless, accurate, and fun. With FitTrack, thousands of users have already reached their personal fitness milestones.

Key Terms

BMI
Body Mass Index - a measure of body fat based on height and weight.
Macros
Short for macronutrients: proteins, carbohydrates, and fats.
Streak
The number of consecutive days a user has logged an activity in FitTrack.
